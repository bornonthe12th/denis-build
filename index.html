NO SUPERHERO ORIGIN. JUST A LOT OF CODE.

It started
with a problem.

In 2009, I was working on reporting systems. The lesson was simple: when software fails, someone's real day gets harder.

Then came my first integrations. Vehicle data. Business systems. External services. Suddenly, the interesting part wasn't just the code — it was what happened between things.

That curiosity has followed me through different industries, stacks and teams.

The foundations

Reporting systems taught me about reliability. Integrating external vehicle data taught me something else: the most interesting problems often live between two systems.
