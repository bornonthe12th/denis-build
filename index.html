How I built it ↘

Word Duel looks simple on the surface. Underneath, it’s a real-time multiplayer system where every move must be fair, even when two players act at once or a connection drops.

01
One source of truth

Firebase Cloud Functions validate moves on the server, inside Realtime Database transactions. Clients can display the game, but they cannot rewrite the authoritative room state.

02
Rules that survive concurrency

Dictionary checks, exact letter changes, turn ownership, deadlines and previously used words are enforced together. A shared used-word set persists across rematches.

03
Computer opponents without shortcuts

The bot plays through the same server-side rules as a human. Easy, medium and hard modes vary think time and move strategy rather than bypassing validation.

04
The details matter

Server-defined turn deadlines, scheduled cleanup, dictionary-based word meanings, and idempotent match-result recording keep the experience consistent.

Vite · JavaScript · Firebase Auth · Realtime Database · Cloud Functions · WordNet
