01 / BEHIND THE SCENES

Make the pieces
work together.

The best integration is one the person using it never has to think about.

Across telecoms and energy, I've worked on connecting services, supplier APIs, customer journeys and event-driven systems. Different providers can have very different rules. The challenge is to make the experience feel like one coherent product.

THE PROBLEM

Many suppliers.
Many rules.

Different inputs, responses and timing requirements. A customer shouldn't have to understand any of that.

THE ENGINEERING

One sensible
interface.

Normalise responses, validate data, publish events and make changes safe. Keep the complexity where it belongs.
